# Cows and Bulls Exercises

## Background

Cows and Bulls is a classic code-breaking game where the objective is to correctly guess a random number. We will be required to reverse engineer a binary that plays a guessing game. Winning may not be as easy as it seems... The game tells you how many Cows you have aka right number but wrong spot, and how many bulls you have right number right spot when four digit combinations are entered.

#### Solving the Challenge

I started by running the binary and inputting each 4 number sequence in sequential order to see which combos led to potential Bulls and Cows.

![](Attachments/Pasted%20image%2020261014115913.png)

From the first guess to the second we know we must contain a `1` somewhere in the final combination as the 2 potential right numbers decreased to `1` after removing it, we can also say we will not have a 5.

When we introduce the `6` our potential right numbers goes back up to 2 meaning we must have a `6` somewhere in the mix. We can also say there is no 2 in the final combination.

Moving on from this the next guess `4567` doesn't change the amount of potential right numbers. We can't make any definitive assumptions off of this guess as we could have a 3 and 7 in the final combo so we move on.

Once the `4` gets bumped off and `8` gets brought in our potential right answers decreases meaning `4` must be in the final guess. Now that we have 3 potentially right numbers we can rule out the `3` and `7`. So we know our final combination must include `146` and not `23578`.

Bringing in the `9` count goes up again and that is our final number needed we just need to find where it may go.

Now we look at the bulls. `7890` has one bull meaning `xx9x` must be right. `4` must be in the last position as it was the only one with a bull `xx94`. `6789` had 1 bull and `1` cow, since we know where the `9` goes we can assume the bull was for the `6` and with that we get the combination `6194`.

Typing the combination in as our last guess we obtain the flag.
